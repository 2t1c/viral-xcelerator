# Style Guide

Locked writing rules for all lessons, documents, and content in this repo.

## Formatting

- Maximum two sentences per paragraph.
- Generous line breaks. White space is a feature.
- Bullet points over prose lists.
- No em dashes. Use commas, periods, or parentheses.

## Voice

- No AI-tells. Kill choppy fragment stacking, "it's not X, it's Y" tics, and over-hedging.
- Seamless transitions between ideas. Every tweet or paragraph should hand off to the next.
- Write to one person, not an audience.

## Thread-specific

- Tweets under 280 characters, ideally under 230.
- Cliffhanger or open-loop endings between tweets.
- Hook: rigid, follow proven templates. Body and ending: flexible, your voice.
- Deliver on the hook's promise. It's only clickbait if you don't.

## Brand aesthetic

- Premium, minimalist, restrained color, strong contrast.
- Visuals follow the Media Finding Playbook quality bar.
