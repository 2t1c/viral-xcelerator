# Tool Cheatsheet

Every tool here has a free plan. Start at $0 and upgrade as needed. Device-wise, anything that can store media and process simple videos is plenty (a MacBook M2 16GB is more than enough).

| Tool | Price | Best for | Importance |
|------|-------|----------|------------|
| **Microsoft Edge** | Free | Writing browser — sidebar ribbon kills context-switching, keeps one tab pinned while you work | ⭐⭐⭐ Essential |
| **Typefully** | Free / $8 mo | Drafting, scheduling, and analyzing X threads; Pro adds unlimited scheduling + AI writing | ⭐⭐⭐ Essential |
| **Claude (Anthropic)** | Free / $20 mo | Deep research + content generation; Pro = faster, better models, higher limits | ⭐⭐⭐ Essential |
| **Notion** | Free / $8 mo | Content calendar, notes, asset management; Pro = big uploads, AI features | ⭐⭐⭐ Essential |
| **4K Video Downloader+** | Free / $15–65 one-time | Batch downloads, hi-res saving, subtitle extraction for repurposing | ⭐⭐ Helpful |
| **Opus Clips** | Free / $19 mo | Auto-cuts long videos into social-ready short clips | ⭐⭐ Helpful |
| **ChatGPT** | Free / $20 mo | Ideation, editing, brainstorming; Pro unlocks GPT-4 + speed | ⭐⭐ Helpful |
| **Perplexity AI** | Free / $20 mo | Source-backed research + fact-checking (Google alternative) | ⭐⭐ Helpful |
| **OBS Studio** | Free | Screen recording + live streaming, no limits | ⭐ Optional |
| **Savefrom.net** | Free | Quick video downloads from YouTube/X (limited on some platforms) | ⭐ Optional |
| **Pinterest** | Free | Visual bookmarking for ideas + content references | ⭐ Optional |
| **Paid retweets** | $10–30 / post | Boosting reach on specific posts via networks | ⭐ Optional |

## Budget tiers

| Budget | What you run |
|--------|--------------|
| **$0** | Free tools + manual effort + engagement group |
| **$80–240/mo** | Add paid boosts for faster reach |
| **$300/mo** | Every tool at full capacity + boosts |

The three-star tools are the daily-driver stack. Two-star tools save time once you're producing volume. One-star is situational.
