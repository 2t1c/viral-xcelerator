# Session 1 — Kim: Workflow & Templates

**Goal:** understand the current workflow, find quick wins, then bigger changes. Performance first; quantity only matters if quality stays consistent (otherwise → burnout, stalled growth).

## Current pain points
- Pastes research into Claude, asks for a thread, then heavily edits. Output sounds AI ("not X, but Y", over-justification, generic phrasing). Claude's context window forces restarts on long sessions.
- A thread takes ~2 hours; **editing/shaping** is the biggest sink.

## Time targets
- Threads (AI-drafted + media): ~1 hr or under. Long-form: ~30 min. Media: under 1 hr now, pushable to 15–30 min.

## The core method: templates + structure vs. information
- Find hooks that already went viral; break each into **structure (stays)** and **information (swap)**.
- The whole method is two small additions to "paste into AI and rewrite": (1) knowing *which template* to hand the AI, (2) knowing *what to rewrite vs. keep*.
- Use judgment on what to change vs. keep. Keep hooks high-level: build curiosity, don't assume, don't add new info.

## Hooks by difficulty
- **History is the hardest niche** for hooks (too much info to compress) — unless you use a rigid proven structure, which makes it easy and repeatable.
- Easy-to-adapt = story templates. Hard = heavily personalized to a specific person/story (needs much more editing).
- **Get the idea/angle right and everything is easier.** An inherently intriguing idea carries the thread.
- **Authority association**: lean on authority/association rather than naming the subject early.

## Viral phrases
Reusable proven lines ("if you thought the pyramids were impressive, this will blow your mind", "you've been lied to", "what they created next…"). Store in a doc; drop in when stuck. **Sensationalized framing** as a rule: "biggest/greatest/most ___ in history."

## Using AI for hooks
Prefer ChatGPT for the long context. Collect 20–30 hooks from your page + strong accounts, paste into one chat, have it learn the structure, then generate.

## Reducing rewrites
Set clear rules up front; personalize the AI; feed approved reference threads; build a master prompt that fixes recurring edits so you start from a "V2."

## Length & format
- Character count matters more than line count. Sweet spot ~220–260 chars.
- Hooks usually 3–4 sentences, no sentence >2 lines, ~3–4 lines total (not a hard rule; bullets can break it up).
- Always deliver on the hook's promise. Every length has an audience — pick by preference and brand.

## Media
Edge + Typefully sidebar; Pinterest native search + Google Images; layers (images first, videos second); text snippets/screenshots count. Thumbnails: one clear emotion matching the hook, minimal editing.

## Distribution note
Paid retweets + bigger accounts resharing cushion the dips, so underperformance for a few days is partly covered by outside distribution.
