## Quote-retweet (QRT) strategy — main tip
- Writing a new post? Paste the link to a past post so it gets QRT'd underneath. Old post's audience sees the new one; impressions transfer **both ways** (two-way sync).
- Works for long-form and threads. Feels natural (real users do it to add a thought).
- QRT **your own** post (build an ecosystem/funnel — readers go post→post) or **someone else's** related post (adds credibility; you didn't invent the topic).
- Few writers do it → an edge.

## Long-form hooks & the 280-char cutoff
- Only the first ~280 chars show on the timeline; the rest is under "Show more."
- Apply viral-hook discipline to the long-form opening; land a compelling line — ideally the CTA/value line — **before** the cutoff so people click "Show more."
- Workflow: paste text, watch the char count, test ~235 vs ~314 to see where it truncates, arrange the hook to fit. "Long-form doesn't mean the hook isn't important."

## Media philosophy refinements
- Every visual adds **friction** (stop → consume → restart). If a passage flows, you don't *have* to add media.
- More media ≠ better; too much can hurt. Cut media to keep people reading to the end.
