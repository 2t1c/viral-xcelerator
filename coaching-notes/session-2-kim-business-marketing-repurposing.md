# Session 2 — Kim: Business/Marketing, Repurposing & Hook Craft

## Posting time & consistency
- The right *window* beats the exact time. Most consumption is wake-up → lunch. US/East Coast (EST): ~7am–1pm their time.
- Retweet network factor: most boosting vendors are in India/Pakistan — posting past midnight your time means they're asleep and the post misses its boost ~12 hrs.
- Practical window: 7–11pm Vietnam (8pm–12am Philippines). 1–2 hrs off is fine. Align demographic with your team.

## Repurposing old content
- No fixed rule. Rough guide: >7 days okay, >14 days safer. Same-day reposts perform independently; audiences rarely notice.
- Low-view originals (~2k) = essentially no one saw it, so no fatigue concern — only question is permission/frequency.
- **Skill flex:** repurpose a *flop* into a winner — proves skill, builds a performing-asset library, earns repurposing freedom.

## Repurposing workflow (McDonald's Monopoly / Jacobson example)
1. Before writing, gather context — search the niche for the same story written by others.
2. Collect **viral phrases** into a swipe section ("the perfect promotion", "single human error", "biggest disaster in marketing history", "one X mistake brought Y to its knees").
3. Build the new hook *from* proven material. Gets you off a blank page.

## Why viral phrases work
Little info, but they add exaggeration and stakes that make a "dead"/irrelevant story feel worth caring about. Repurposing = bringing a dead story back to life via primal triggers.

## Hook principles (from notes)
No assumption (most people won't decode a reference) · target primal feelings (revenge/envy/jealousy — like news headlines) · active voice + vivid scene · viral phrases + adverbs to intensify · prioritize & place (the one prime-real-estate fact).

## Hook teardown (Jacobson)
- Don't name too early; lead with intrigue.
- Cut weak words ("promotion"?); "of all time" → "in marketing history."
- Don't over-give (a Monopoly-piece reference the reader may not get) — cut it.
- Keep strong specifics ("12 years, $24M, 51 accomplices").
- Replace flat tags; CTA line is the most flexible.
- The gap between 2k and 2M views is often just word choice + what you foreground.

## Why business/marketing is hardest
Business is boring by default; no established native audience (operators don't read this content). Health/psychology = the reader's own pain; history = built-in story community.

## Business → marketing content
- Simplest: paste the post into Claude, ask it to relate to marketing.
- **Broaden "marketing" to include branding/positioning** — authority, reputation, perception. Keep the story, link the back half to brand/marketing outcomes, then "here's how you apply it."
- Study thought-leader accounts that connect ordinary business stories to brand/marketing importance.

## Workflow tips
Structure-first hooks (give Claude the structure to fill, don't let it invent). Research while the AI drafts. Prompt specificity helps but manual editing of the hook is unavoidable — volume builds the speed.
