# Session 3 — Kimmi: Media Finding & Easy-Niche Hooks

**Who:** psychology/philosophy writer, ex-Facebook/LinkedIn advocacy content, new to X. Threads take 3–5 hrs, media is the biggest sink; unsure what counts as "premium." Writes with dashes → reads as AI.

## Why psychology/philosophy is the *easiest* niche
Proven viral hook templates + health's problem→solution structure. A psychology thread = a mental-health thread: name/diagnose a problem, present a solution. Media is *not* hard here. Philosophy ≈ psychology (transferable templates).

## Media — the mindset shift
**Finding media is never the hard part.** Once you accept that, the problem flips from "can't find anything" to "too many options — what style?"
- Media isn't only images: article snippets, AI-answer snippets, headline/study screenshots, quotes, diagrams, tweets all count.
- Work in layers (onion): first pass isn't final, get the big picture, don't perfect any one choice. Nothing is final from the start.
- Don't get stuck — if it won't copy or doesn't fit, move on.
- Images first, videos second.

## Media toolkit
- **Edge + pinned Typefully sidebar + split screen**; one-click download extension; drag into the thread. Saves ~30 min/thread.
- **X/Twitter search → Media tab** (underrated; already on-platform + relevant).
- Pinterest, Google Images, YouTube (download shorts/reels).
- **Opus Pro** to chop one long video into 5–10 clips (~30–60s).
- Abstract aesthetic visuals (search "aesthetic", "journal", "dark academia") pair with a quote/snippet — don't use alone.
- Frame an image into a square (black bg) so it sits next to text cleanly — often removes the need for Canva.

## How much media + ratio
No more than 3 tweets in a row without media. Target ~8:2 images:videos (flex to 7:3, 6:4). Media must earn its place (info or design value). "Close enough" abstract images work in psychology.

## "Premium" aesthetic
Clean, minimalist, modern, controlled color, contrast (dark/light), plus the message behind the image. Avoid AI-looking images. The "eye" develops with reps; ping the group when unsure. Reorder bullets by length (ascending).

## Time target
In the groove: media for a whole thread = **10–15 min**.

## Hooks (psychology/philosophy)
- Be proactive: pick the proven hook to replicate, hand the AI the structure, adapt keeping ~90% similarity. The 90% is structure; the rest is your info.
- Bracket what changes — often just the figure's name.
- Prime real estate: one line carries the weight; word choice matters most there.
- Rule of three + wide net (stress/anxiety/depression).
- Build a personal phrase bank → within a month you can assemble organic-looking hooks from proven parts.

## Hook vs CTA
Hook (first lines) is rigid; final/CTA line is where you flex. One word reframes the whole thread ("Here's how" → "Here's the pattern"/"the mindset shift"). Mantra: even if it's simple, it's not easy.

## Coaching boundary
Gave directional feedback tied to principles, not rewrites — they must build their own discretion.
