# 04 — Monetization

You have attention. This module turns it into income. Two doors, each its own course. Pick based on your strengths and goals. Some students run both.

- `digital-products.md` — an ebook, template, or community. Leveraged income: build it once, sell it forever. Best if you want to monetize your own audience.
- `service-based.md` — ghostwriting, AI system builds, distribution, consulting, cohorts. Higher touch, higher revenue per client. Best if you want cashflow from other people's budgets.

## Which door first?

- **Newer creator, smaller audience:** start with a **free digital product**. It builds your email list and warms buyers while you keep learning the craft. Low risk, pure upside.
- **Strong writer who wants cashflow now:** **ghostwriting** pays fastest because your own page is your portfolio.
- **Either way, the skill stack is the same.** Everything you learned in the Thread Masterclass is what makes the product good or the client results real.

Both paths assume you can already write a thread. If you cannot yet, finish `02-thread-masterclass/` first.
