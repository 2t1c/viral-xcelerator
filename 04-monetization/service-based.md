## Foundation — Your Options and the Real Economics

There are five service paths. Pick based on what you can actually deliver, not the highest sticker price.

| Service | Typical pricing | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Ghostwriting | $2-4k/mo per client | Strong writers with taste |
| AI system build | $5-10k one-time | Technical operators who build pipelines |
| RT distribution | $300-1k/mo per client | Creators with large followings or networks |
| Consulting | $200-500/hr | Creators with proven, repeatable results |
| Community / cohort | $500-2k per seat | Operators who want to scale teaching |

> Pricing reflects ranges taught in the program. Your rates depend on your skill, niche, and results.

**Ghostwriting** is the most natural service for a strong writer. Charge per thread, not per hour. Most ghostwriters handle 2-4 clients before quality drops.

**The hidden cost most people forget: distribution.** A client expects performance, and a thread at 5,000 views does not move a brand. Hitting 100k+ usually needs paid retweets, and those cost money.

Price it in. A $3k/mo engagement that quietly needs $1k of RTs is really $1.5k/mo. Either the client pays for RTs separately, you bundle them in (it eats margin), or the client has their own distribution.

**Leverage check.** A $3k/mo ghostwriting client at 20 hours is $150/hour. A $400 consulting call is $400/hour. The numbers tell you which path is high-leverage, which is why most operators stack two or three services.

**Agency vs independent.** Agencies give stability and let you focus on craft, but cap your pay. Independent means higher pay and full ownership, but you run every business function. Many start at an agency to build skill and a portfolio, then go independent.

## Positioning — Build an Offer That Sells Itself

Done right, ghostwriting is a no-brainer offer because you can tie it directly to the business results the client cares about.

- **Set up the offer** on the universal principles first: a clear outcome, a clear who-it's-for, and a reason it beats the alternative.
- **Construct the ghostwriting offer** around the system you run (research, ideation, drafting, editing) applied to the client's voice. The client is buying the system, not just words.
- **Underpromise and overdeliver.** Structure the offer with the value equation in mind so you can command premium rates.
- **Become a premium Genius Thread ghostwriter.** You only need two things to start in any industry: proof you can write threads that perform, and a specific offer aimed at a specific buyer.

## Attracting Clients — The Fastest Path

The fastest route to a premium client is to take the risk upfront with strategic free work.

- Write one exceptional free thread for a carefully chosen prospect, or hand them 5 tailored thread ideas.
- Let the results speak, then book a call to discuss working together.
- Do the ROI math: if 1-2 hours of free work lands a client worth $4,500/mo for 3-4 months, you write the thread.

**Work smarter.** You do not need unique content per prospect. Write one great thread, make small variations, and customize the CTA per prospect.

**Fill the top of the funnel too:**
- Build a portfolio (your own page counts).
- Post content pillars that generate inbound leads.
- Study examples of lead-gen threads: they must make the reader believe you specifically are worth hiring.
- Qualify leads before the call (business size and budget are the key filters), then run the sales call with a proven framework (the CLOSER approach in the ghostwriting module).
- Use strategic freework as long-term positioning, not a giveaway.

## Fulfillment — Deliver and Retain

An efficient workflow is what lets you juggle multiple clients without quality dropping.

- Lean on lower-risk hooks with proven performance patterns (risk-o-meter: client work is risk 1-2).
- Keep a library of reliable formats you can adapt per industry.
- Outsource time-intensive tasks like media finding and reporting.
- Send a short brief per thread (main idea, reference source, forecasted performance, certainty, notes) so the client has certainty over performance.

**Tools:** Typefully for drafting, scheduling, and delivery. Slack for ongoing client conversation. Beeper to consolidate multiple chat platforms into one inbox.

**Retention runs on trust.** Report results in business terms, collect testimonials that speak to business impact (not just view counts), and communicate like you are thinking about the whole business, not just the thread. That is what makes a client value the service and stay.

## The right mix

Most successful operators stack services: consulting for the high hourly rate, 2-3 ghostwriting clients for recurring revenue, and RT distribution as an add-on. That gets you to $10-20k/mo within a few clients without overloading any single delivery channel.
