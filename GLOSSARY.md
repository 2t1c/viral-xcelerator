# Glossary

Internal language used across the program and coaching notes.

- **Prime real estate** — the one line in a hook where all the value concentrates. Every word in it fights for its life.
- **Inner-CTA** — the last line of a hook, reframed as the question that sits just before the thread's most interesting point. Changes how the whole post is perceived.
- **Negotiable vs Non-Negotiable** — the house analogy. Non-negotiables are a template's frame (don't touch). Negotiables are the furniture (the information you swap). Bracket the negotiables.
- **Twist the knife** — sharpening a line with a power word or contrarian angle so it cuts deeper ("were never private" → "the disturbing truth about").
- **Viral phrase** — a proven line that adds little information but improves rhythm, structure, or timeline presence ("once you see it, you can't unsee it").
- **Power words** — emotionally loaded words that recur in viral hooks because they work: disturbing, shocking, spy, surveilled, playbook, protocol.
- **Risk-o-meter** — how much a post needs to perform. Own pages = playground (high risk OK). Client work = risk 1-2 (proven templates only). "Everyone's an artist until rent is due."
- **Rule of One** — one big idea, one target audience, one takeaway, one CTA per post.
- **What-Why-How** — the universal spine. What = the big idea. Why = why the reader should care (the heavy lifting). How = the lessons, story, or steps.
- **Contrast principle** — the gap between outcome and starting point drives intrigue (pizza boy → $1.3B company).
- **Authority association** — borrowing credibility by naming respected people, firms, or institutions. Count 3 or 5, never 4.
- **Open loop** — curiosity deliberately left unresolved in the hook, closed inside the thread. Closing it early kills the click.
- **Placeholder method** — leaving "[add your experience]" slots in client drafts for them to fill, then editing.
- **Swipe file** — a categorized library of proven hooks and phrases, bracketed into structure vs information.
- **85-90% rule** — keep that much structural similarity to a proven template when results matter. 95% risks being called out, loose adaptation loses the certainty.
- **Genius Thread** — the premium tier: 12-18 tweets, strong narrative, clear outcome, ≤2 tweets without media, premium visuals.
- **Wholesaler** — a single retweet vendor who manages the whole spread.
- **QRT** — quote-retweet, used deliberately as a distribution and ecosystem tactic.
- **Informative image** — a visual that answers the follow-up question a reader has after the tweet.
- **Clipper / Context-related / Video-heavy / Movie** — the four thread media types.
