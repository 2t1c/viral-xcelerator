# The Components (Profile Setup)

Work through these in order. The whole setup should take twenty to thirty minutes, so don't overthink it.

## Profile photo

- For a **theme page**, pick an image that anchors the niche.
- For a **personal account**, a clean stylized portrait or sharp headshot works.
- Whatever you choose, it has to read clearly at a small size — that's how the timeline displays it.

## Banner

- Most creators waste this space. Use it to reinforce the page's identity.
- **Theme page:** pair a tagline with a stylized image.
- **Personal account:** lean abstract or aesthetic.
- The banner sets the mood for everything below it.

## Display name and handle

- The **name** should be the cleanest version of what the page is about.
- The **handle** should be memorable and easy to type.
- Don't get clever for the sake of clever.

## Bio

The most important text on your profile, and the most neglected. It has three jobs:

- Tell visitors **who** the page is for.
- Tell them **what** they'll get by following.
- Give them a reason to **act now** — a credential, a number, or a link.

See `examples/geniusgtx-teardown.md` for a worked example of all four components together.
