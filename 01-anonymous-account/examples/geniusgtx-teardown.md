# Profile Teardown: @GeniusGTX

A clean example of the components working together.

## Profile photo

- **Einstein** instantly anchors the niche — genius, intellect, big minds.
- Reads clearly at timeline size: high contrast, recognizable silhouette, no clutter.

## Banner

- "beauty and brains" in elegant script over a minimalist bird-on-branch illustration.
- Reinforces identity and sets a refined, intellectual mood. Not wasted space.

## Display name and handle

- **GeniusThinking** says exactly what the page is about — the cleanest version of the idea.
- **@GeniusGTX** is short, memorable, easy to type.

## Bio (the standout)

Hits all three jobs cleanly:

- **Who it's for:** people drawn to the greatest minds in economics, psychology, and history.
- **What you get:** understand how the world works through human genius.
- **Reason to act now:** 278K+ followers as social proof, plus a direct Gumroad link to the mental models product.

## The takeaway

Every block pulls in the same direction — niche, mood, promise, proof, and a clear next step. Nothing is decorative; nothing is wasted. That coherence is what the follower count is responding to.

> **One refinement:** the bio pushes people to follow the same account they're already viewing ("Follow @GeniusGTX"), which is slightly redundant when read on the profile itself — likely a leftover from cross-posting. Minor, but worth tightening.
