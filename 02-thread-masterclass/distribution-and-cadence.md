# Distribution & Cadence Reference

## Retweets (the current engine)

Threads still need retweets for the initial traction. The platform is moving toward better organic reach over the next 6 to 12 months, but for now distribution is part of the equation.

Budget tiers:

- **Beginner:** $15 to 20/day is a workable start. Balance cash flow against volume.
- **Solid:** $10 to 20/day still gets good results for some.
- **Removes distribution from the equation:** $40 to 50/day. At this level, focus purely on content quality.

Buy through a **wholesaler** (one person manages the spread) instead of juggling multiple vendors. Easier cash flow, one relationship.

**Risk warning:** X appears to be cracking down on retweet vendors. Accounts have received "manipulating the platform" notices after vendor retweets. Watch this space.

## Promo button (native ads)

Mixed results so far:

- Boosted too early, it can buy ~50k empty views (~$25/day) at near-zero CTR. X distributes the boost unevenly, and a bad early CTR can kill the post.
- Boosted after a post is already gaining: one ~3M organic post was pushed to ~6M. Another went to ~1M on $30 to 40 spend and drove ~$1k in sales.
- Working theory: let the post prove itself with retweets first, then add promo to a winner. Timing is still being tested.
- Long term, native promo may be safer than vendors if the crackdown continues.

**Engagement groups** mostly don't work on X anymore. Skip them.

## Posting cadence

- Typical brand-account load: 3 posts/day per account (one video, one long-form or thread, one transcript post).
- **Posting window:** 7 to 11pm Vietnam time hits EST mornings, and aligns with when retweet vendors (often India/Pakistan timezones) are active.
- Ideally hold one week of scheduled content. Day-by-day is survivable but fragile.
- Typefully is the single source of truth. No parallel project-management boards unless the volume demands it.

## Repurposing rules

- Repurpose after **7+ days** (safe), **14+ days** (safer).
- A flop the first time isn't the last time. Each repost, change one lever: format, hook, or thumbnail.
- Winners are assets. The more proven assets a page holds, the easier future posting gets.

## Time targets

- Long-form post: 15 to 30 minutes.
- Thread: 1 hour max (premium psychology baseline: ~30 min — scripting 5-10, media 5-10, the rest on the hook).
- Articles: in between, flexible.
