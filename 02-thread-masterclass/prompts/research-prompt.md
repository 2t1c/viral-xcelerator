# Research Prompt (Find Interesting Angles)

Paste your idea into the **Big Idea** placeholder. This gathers raw material — synthesis comes later.

```
Big Idea: [Insert Thread Topic or Main Question From Step 1]

---

Description: We're looking for detailed, fascinating information to create a viral Twitter thread about [Thread Topic] for our brand. It should be well-researched, engaging, and full of unique insights our audience of curious, ambitious learners would find valuable.

Please answer each question with as much detail and specificity as possible:

1. What are the key events, figures, or concepts related to [Thread Topic]? Give a timeline or historical context if relevant.
2. Any surprising or little-known facts, anecdotes, or case studies? The more specific and unusual, the better. Go deep.
3. Any exclusive quotes, firsthand accounts, or expert perspectives that can't be found elsewhere?
4. Any thought-provoking debates or controversies we could explore to drive engagement?
5. How does [Thread Topic] connect to bigger themes the audience cares about — personal development, innovation, psychology, societal impact?
6. Any practical lessons, strategies, or action steps readers could apply to their own lives? The more concrete, the better.
7. Any striking visuals, infographics, or media that would strengthen the storytelling?
8. What sources or references would you suggest for further reading and fact-checking?

Please be as comprehensive as possible, and add anything else you think would help craft a remarkable thread.
```
