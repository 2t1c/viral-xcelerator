# Metrics & Benchmarks

## View benchmarks (current algorithm)

- **Insane:** over 500k
- **Good:** 50k to 200k
- **Horrifying:** under 10k

Note: the algo is in flux. Even great CTR + retention no longer guarantees a boost. Control the controllables: hook quality and distribution.

## The two core metrics

1. **Click-through rate (CTR).** Over 5% = strong hook. The main signal of how the content was perceived, and the main thing to optimize.
2. **Retention.** Over 50% = the body is delivering on the hook's promise.

## Secondary signals

- **Bookmark-to-like ratio.** Bookmarks higher than likes = high perceived value (educational, psychology, business content). But not every hook is built to be bookmarked. Some are built to be opened (curiosity), some to spark comments (controversy). The best combine all three naturally.
- **Internal engagement.** Meaningful comments resurface the thread. Controversial angles drive conversation. Reply to comments after posting to keep the fire going.
- **"Ask Grok to verify" comments** = a signal of real, organic engagement.

## What each hook style optimizes

- **Curiosity / open loop** → CTR (people click in, don't bookmark).
- **High perceived value** → bookmarks.
- **Controversy** → comments and conversation.

Match the hook style to the objective before writing.
