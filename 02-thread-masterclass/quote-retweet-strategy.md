# Quote-Retweet (QRT) Strategy

A free, no-downside way to recycle attention and build a content ecosystem. Few creators use it deliberately, so it's an edge.

## How it works

Write your post as normal in Typefully. Paste the link to another post at the bottom. It renders as a quote-retweet underneath.

Two modes:

1. **QRT your own past post.** The old post's audience gets shown the new one, and impressions transfer **both ways**. A two-way sync that helps both posts perform.
2. **QRT someone else's related post** (ideally one already performing). You borrow its distribution, and it adds credibility: you didn't invent the topic, it's already being discussed. Your post reads as a fresh take on a live conversation.

## Why it compounds

- Readers move from one connected post to the next. They stay inside your page's ecosystem and go down the rabbit hole.
- The page feels alive and human. Real users QRT to add a thought on top of existing posts, so it looks organic.
- The QRT process is identical to writing a long-form post. Write the post, paste the link, done.

## Tips

- Add a small CTA above the QRT if you want ("if you enjoyed this, read the one below to understand X").
- Works for long-form posts, threads, and plain tweets.
- The two posts must connect logically in the reader's mind. If you can link them, it's good enough. Too far apart and it breaks.
- You can tell the AI: "turn this into a commentary based on this quote-retweet" to slightly rewrite an existing draft for QRT framing.
- You can even QRT something in a thread hook to borrow traffic at the hook level.
