# The Hook Is Where You Get Rigid

The hook is where angle, density, and curiosity concentrate into a single line.

It's the highest-leverage sentence you'll write. Most readers decide whether to keep going before they finish it. A great post with a weak hook is a great post nobody reads.

So here's the counterintuitive part: **the hook is the one place you should not try to be original.**

After the hook, you have room — for your voice, your story, your angle. But the hook itself follows patterns that already work, and as a beginner, originality there is a tax you can't afford. You don't invent hooks. You model them.

## Why You Steal the Pattern First

Every niche has a small set of hook structures that reliably stop the scroll. They work because they map onto hardwired drives: fear, status, curiosity, the search for an edge.

Inventing a new structure means betting your post on an untested pattern. Modeling a proven one means starting from something the market has already rewarded. You're not copying words — you're copying the *skeleton* and refilling it with your topic.

## The Method: Swipe and Vary

1. **Find proven hooks in your niche.** Pull from posts that actually performed (high replies + reposts relative to account size), recent enough that the market hasn't gone numb to them.
2. **Reverse-engineer why it worked.** What drive does it hit? What tension does it open? Who is it for? If you can't explain it, you can't reuse it.
3. **Strip it to a template.** Delete the topic, keep the structure.
   > "I went from $400k in debt to selling for $46M, here's the part nobody talks about"
   > → "I went from [bad start] to [big outcome], here's the part nobody talks about"
4. **Refill with your topic.** The structure carries the weight; your content makes it specific.
5. **Generate variations, then pick.** Write 8–10 off proven structures, choose the sharpest. The first hook is rarely the best.

## What Strong Hooks Share

- **Specificity.** A real number, name, or situation. "$46M" beats "a lot of money."
- **An open loop.** They promise something they don't immediately deliver.
- **Reader-state mirroring.** They name where the reader is sitting right now.
- **Raised stakes.** They make the topic feel like it matters more than assumed.

## Weak vs Strong

| Weak Hook | Strong Hook |
|-----------|-------------|
| "Here are some productivity tips." | "I wasted 3 years on productivity systems. One 5-minute habit replaced all of them." |
| "Marketing is important for business." | "A founder I know spent $0 on ads and hit $2M. He did one thing differently." |
| "Let's talk about discipline." | "The most disciplined people I've met aren't disciplined at all. Here's what they do instead." |

## Build a Swipe File

Keep a running file of hooks that stopped *you* mid-scroll. Tag each by structure (story, contrarian, list, authority, mistake). Within a month you'll have a personal library of proven patterns and never start from a blank page again. A simple Notion database with columns for the hook, source, structure type, and why it worked is enough. (See `../swipe-files/`.)

## Common Mistakes

- Writing the hook last, as an afterthought. It's the most important line — spend the most time here.
- Being clever instead of clear. Clarity stops scrolls; cleverness rarely does.
- Copying the words, not the structure.
- Settling for the first draft.

## Hook Generator Prompt

```
You are an expert X hook writer. I'll give you (1) my topic and (2) a few proven hooks from my niche. Model their structures and write fresh hooks for my topic.

1. For each proven hook, identify its underlying structure (strip the topic, keep the skeleton) and name the drive it hits (fear, status, curiosity, edge).
2. Using those structures, write 10 hook variations for MY topic. Reuse the proven skeletons — do not invent new structures.
3. Every hook must: be specific (real number, name, or situation), open a loop the reader has to close, and mirror where the reader sits right now.
4. Rank all 10 strongest to weakest, and in one line each, explain why the top 3 work.

No vague hooks, no clever wordplay that needs decoding. Clarity over cleverness.

MY TOPIC: [your topic]
PROVEN HOOKS FROM MY NICHE:
[paste 3–5 hooks here]
```
