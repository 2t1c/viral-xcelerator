# Start Here: Talk to the Course with AI

This whole repo is built to be read by an AI coach. Add it to your chat and the course can answer you directly, grade your drafts, and walk you through any lesson on demand. This page shows you how to set it up and what to ask.

---

## 1. Load the coach

**In Claude Code (recommended):**
- Add this repository to your session.
- The coach persona in `CLAUDE.md` loads automatically. Just start asking.

**In ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude.ai, or anywhere else:**
- Open `AI-COACH.md`, copy the prompt block, and paste it as your first message.
- Then paste in whichever lesson file you want help with.

## 2. Tell it where you are

The coach works best when it knows your stage and whether you are writing for your own page or a client. One line is enough:

> "I'm on my own anonymous finance page, ~2k followers, trying to write my first viral thread."

## 3. Starter prompts by goal

**I want to write a thread right now**
- "Walk me through `02-thread-masterclass/viral-threads-in-a-box.md` for the idea: [your idea]."
- "Give me three outline options for this idea using the structures in `tweet-structures.md`."
- "Here's my hook. Grade it against the 85-90% rule and the hook mechanics, then show me the fix: [paste hook]."
- "Twist the knife on this line: [paste line]."

**I want to sharpen a draft**
- "Here's my draft thread. Where do people drop off, and which tweet is weakest? [paste]."
- "Is my hook delivering on its promise, or is this clickbait? [paste]."
- "Rewrite tweet 2 so it answers 'why should I care?' without breaking the open loop."

**I want to monetize**
- "I have an audience but no offer. Should I start with a digital product or a service? Walk me through `04-monetization/`."
- "Help me find a product my audience already wants, using `04-monetization/digital-products.md`."
- "Price a ghostwriting offer for a founder client using `04-monetization/service-based.md`."

**I want to scale my output**
- "Set up my content machine from `05-scaling/content-machine.md` so I can batch a week of threads."
- "Which of my past threads should I repurpose, and how? Use `05-scaling/repurposing.md`."

## 4. How to get good answers

- **Paste your real work.** The coach is far more useful grading your hook than answering in the abstract.
- **Ask why.** Make it explain the framework behind every suggestion so you learn the craft, not just the output.
- **Respect the sequence.** If you are not yet fluent at threads, do not jump to monetization. The coach will steer you back, and it is right to.
- **Use the glossary.** If a term is unfamiliar (inner-CTA, prime real estate, negotiables), ask the coach to define it from `GLOSSARY.md`.

## 5. What the coach will not do

- It will not just spit out a finished thread with no teaching. That breaks the one rule of this program.
- It will not promise you a specific number of views or dollars. It coaches the process that gives you the best shot.
