# Viral Phrases

Proven lines collected across coaching sessions. They carry little information on their own, but their phrasing adds stakes, curiosity, or flow. Store every line that stops *you* mid-scroll; over a month you'll have a Lego set for assembling hooks.

> A viral phrase mostly improves structure, rhythm, and how the hook looks on the timeline. Drop one in when a hook feels incomplete or you're stuck.

## Curiosity / open-loop closers (great as Inner-CTAs)
- Once you see it, you can't unsee it.
- The pattern I witnessed blew my mind.
- His last words left me thinking.
- What he did next [exposed / re-wrote the playbook forever].
- Here's how it all unfolded.
- Here's the full story of how ONE [X] mistake brought [Y] to its knees.
- This crisis is worse than ever.
- You've been lied to.

## Scale / superlative framing
- The [biggest / greatest / strangest / worst] [X] in [Y] history.
- The most [dangerous / glorified / overlooked] [problem] in the world.
- The [youngest / oldest] [billionaire] you've never heard of.
- [X] is the new [Y].  (e.g. "Ryan Reynolds is the new Steve Jobs")

## Effort / authority / transformation
- I spent [X] hours / For [X] years I've been obsessed with [Y]…
- I used to be [X], but now I'm [Y].
- Without [X], you are [Y].
- After [credential, e.g. 2,000 clinical hours]…
- The smartest [founders / people] follow [these patterns].

## "Twist the knife" / contrarian
- They're not an [X] problem, they're a [Y] problem.
- No amount of [X] will save you.
- The [disturbing truth / real reason] about [X].
- Not [obvious thing], but [unexpected thing].

## Power keywords (swap into the prime-real-estate line)
Strategy · system · playbook · protocol · disturbing · shocking · spy / surveilled · big tech · top executives · study · without · obsessed.

## Psychology wide-net keywords
anxiety · stress · depression · trapped · overthinking · nervous system. (Naming three at once casts a wider net — most readers resonate with at least one.)

---

**Build your own:** categorize by use case (curiosity, authority, transformation, contrarian, scale). For client work, keep a per-client bank — phrases that have resonated with *their* audience, reusable across their threads.
