# Power Words and Metrics

**Purpose:** The quantified layer behind the swipe file. Every claim here is grounded in the engagement numbers on real thread openers, so the writing rules are backed by evidence rather than taste.

**Sample:** The 32 curated hooks in [viral-thread-examples.md](./viral-thread-examples.md), each above 5M views, plus a broader scan of 100+ thread openers that each cleared 1M views. Counts below are from the curated 32 unless noted.

For the structural templates, see [hook-pattern-library.md](./hook-pattern-library.md). For dataset-wide percentile benchmarks across all 2,905 verified viral threads (CTR, retention, thread length, hook length, niche performance), see [benchmark-evidence.md](../02-thread-masterclass/benchmark-evidence.md).

## The save-rate vs view-rate split

This is the most useful finding in the set. Bookmark rate (bookmarks divided by views) measures reference value: how often a reader thought "I need this later." It splits hard by hook type.

**Save magnets (utility, list, how-to).** Bookmark rate 0.35% to 1.75%.

| Hook | Niche | Views | Bookmark rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patrick Winston "How to Speak" | Business | 5.1M | 1.75% |
| ChatGPT flight prompts | AI | 11.5M | 0.96% |
| Fasting 72 hours | Health | 21.8M | 0.47% |
| Testosterone foods | Health | 21.7M | 0.46% |
| Barbara O'Neill laws | Health | 19.7M | 0.43% |
| Marketing visuals | Business | 32.4M | 0.41% |
| Seyfried cancer | Health | 26.8M | 0.38% |
| ADHD superpower | Mental Health | 27.1M | 0.38% |
| 24 uncomfortable truths | Life Lessons | 29.8M | 0.35% |

**View magnets (biography, news, spectacle).** Bookmark rate 0.03% to 0.11%.

| Hook | Niche | Views | Bookmark rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Javier Milei | Politics | 66.1M | 0.03% |
| Jaguar rebrand | Business | 35.1M | 0.06% |
| Jason Statham | Business | 64.6M | 0.07% |
| Creative ad copies | Business | 38.1M | 0.08% |
| China Olympics | Finance | 28.7M | 0.10% |
| iShowSpeed | Business | 43.6M | 0.11% |

The gap is roughly 5x to 15x. The lesson: pick the metric you want before you pick the hook. If the goal is reach, a biography or spectacle hook pulls the biggest view counts. If the goal is saves, profile lifts, and a content asset people return to, a numbered how-to or framework list wins even at a fraction of the views.

**When bookmarks beat likes.** In four hooks the bookmark count exceeded the like count, the strongest possible save signal. All four were utility or framework lists: ChatGPT flight prompts (2.6x), Harvard hair regrowth (2.4x), Patrick Winston (2.2x), and the Russian peasant power principle (1.1x). If a draft is meant to be saved, model it on these, and make the promise countable.

## Hook anatomy by the numbers

**The explicit door is near-universal: 31 of 32.** Almost every opener ends on a promise that opens into the body, written as a colon, an ellipsis, or a "Here's..." line. The one hook without a hard door still ended on a forward-pointing line. Treat the door as mandatory.

**A specific number appears in 30 of 32 openers.** Precise figures stop the thumb: "76 death matches", "1 in 7 adults", "$1.79 donation", "30-50%", "228 IQ", "$1,412 flight. I paid $186." Odd specifics outperform round numbers. Two hooks ran on pure curiosity with no number, and both leaned on an extreme claim instead ("This video has changed my life").

**A recognizable anchor appears in 24 of 32.** A known person, brand, or institution buys the first second: Apple, Spotify, Harvard, NASA, MIT, Zuckerberg, Milei, Statham. The familiar name earns attention, then the unexpected angle holds it.

**Line 1 stays short.** Across the set, the scroll-stopping first line runs roughly 4 to 16 words, clustering near 9 to 10. Longer setups exist, but they break into many short lines rather than one long sentence. Keep the first line under about 14 words.

## Power word frequency

Counted across the 32 curated hooks.

| Category | Words | Count |
|---|---|---|
| The door | here's, here are | 13 |
| Superlative | greatest, most, finest, highest, best | 12 |
| Credibility | proven, science, study, scientists, professor | 8 |
| Forbidden knowledge | secret, hidden, banned, erased, concealed, afraid to discuss | 7 |
| Direct address | you, your | 7 |
| Intensity | shocked, stunned, mindblowing, wild, bone-chilling | 6 |
| Negation and inversion | isn't, not, lied | 6 |

Superlatives are never hedged. The set says "the greatest prison break", not "one of the greatest." Write the absolute.

## Niche read

**The very top is History, Business, and Health.** Most hooks above 40M views came from these three. They share a trait: a clear protagonist, a concrete stakes number, and a payoff the reader can picture.

**Politics and spectacle pull huge reach but the lowest saves.** The Milei hook reached 66.1M views with a 0.03% save rate. Reach without retention. Useful for a follower spike, weak for building an evergreen asset.

**AI hooks split by intent.** Demo round-ups ("N mindblowing examples") pull big views. Utility prompts ("7 prompts that break airline pricing") pull elite save rates. The difference is whether the reader can act on it.

**Health and Life Lessons over-index on saves.** Cancer, fasting, testosterone, and "uncomfortable truths" all cleared 0.35%+ bookmark rates. People save what they intend to apply to their own body or life.

## The composite that works

Most hooks in the set stack the same move:

1. One oddly specific number.
2. One recognizable name or institution.
3. One belief reframed or one loop opened.
4. One explicit door into the body.

Then choose the engine by goal. For reach, lead with a protagonist and spectacle. For saves, lead with a countable promise the reader can use later. The numbers say you rarely get both at once, so decide which one the piece is for before you write the first line.
